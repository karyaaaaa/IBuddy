Reassurance Center

IB can feel overwhelming sometimes. That doesn't mean you're doing badly.

🌱

Feeling behind?

Being behind doesn't mean you've failed. Pick one small thing you can work on next, and start there.

💛

Had a bad grade?

One grade doesn't define your ability. Look at what you can learn from it and use that information for your next attempt.

☁️

Can't concentrate?

It's okay to take a short break. Get some water, step away for a moment, then come back and try one small task.

🌷

Everyone seems ahead?

You can't see everything other people are dealing with. Focus on your own progress instead of comparing your journey to someone else's.

🌿

You don't have to do everything today.

Choose one thing. Do that thing. Then decide what comes next. Progress is still progress, even when it's slow.
